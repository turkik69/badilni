Badilni Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Scope

This policy explains how Badilni collects, uses and protects user data. The app is for people aged 13 and over.

Data we collect

We collect your email address, date of birth to check age eligibility, chosen name, listings and photos, swap offers, negotiation messages, reports, ratings and device token if you enable notifications. Hosting services may record technical information necessary for security, such as IP address, browser type and error logs.

How we use data

We use data to create and verify accounts, show listings, run swaps, chats and notifications, prevent abuse, handle reports, protect the service and improve it. We do not sell personal data.

Service providers and sharing

Google Firebase processes authentication, database data and notifications; Cloudflare processes server requests and security. We share data with others only to provide the service, with your consent, or when legally required.

Visibility to other users

Registered users can see listings, photos, descriptions and ratings. Your email address is not shown to other users. Negotiation messages are available to the two participants and to an administrator when needed to investigate a report or abuse.

Retention and security

We retain data while your account remains active or as necessary for reports and legal obligations. We use email verification, server access controls and encrypted connections. No digital service can guarantee absolute security.

Your rights and account deletion

You can edit your name and listings or delete listings in the app. Delete your account and associated data through Profile → Permanently delete account and data. If you cannot sign in, use the account deletion request page. Deletion covers the profile, listings, offers, chats, notifications and related data, subject to limited retention where legally required or to prevent fraud.

Children

People under 13 may not register. If we learn that a child below the minimum age has an account, we will delete the account and its data.
