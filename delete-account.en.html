Delete your Badilni account

You can delete your account and associated data at any time.

Delete directly in the app

  1. Sign in to Badilni.
  2. Open My account.
  3. Select Permanently delete account and data.
  4. Read the notice and confirm.

Deletion starts immediately after confirmation and requires no subscription or payment.

If you cannot sign in

Send a request from the email registered to your account, identifying that address and information that helps verify account ownership. Do not send a password or verification code.

Send deletion request

turki.k69@gmail.com

What is deleted?

  • Authentication account and profile.
  • Listings, photos and swap offers.
  • Chats, notifications and reviews.
  • Points, notification tokens and account related data.

We may retain a limited record only where legally required or necessary to handle a safety report or prevent fraud, then delete it when the purpose ends.
